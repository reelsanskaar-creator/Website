About Vishnu Tools

Welcome to Vishnu Tools, your one-stop solution for all your PDF needs. Our mission is to provide easy-to-use, powerful, and completely free PDF tools that respect your privacy.

In a world where data privacy is paramount, we saw a need for a reliable PDF utility that doesn't require you to upload your sensitive documents to a server. That's why we built this toolkit. Every single operation, from merging documents to adding a password, happens directly within your own web browser. Your files never leave your computer, giving you complete peace of mind.

Our Philosophy

  • Privacy First: We will never ask you to upload your files. All processing is done client-side. What's yours, stays yours.
  • Simplicity: Our tools are designed to be intuitive and straightforward. No complicated interfaces, no unnecessary steps. Just select your tool, choose your file, and get the job done.
  • Accessibility: Vishnu Tools is free for everyone and accessible from any modern web browser.
  • Powerful Functionality: We leverage powerful open-source libraries to provide a robust suite of features that rival many premium desktop applications.

Thank you for choosing Vishnu Tools. We are constantly working to improve our tools and add new features.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 30, 2025

Your privacy is critically important to us. At Vishnu Tools, we have a few fundamental principles:

1. Client-Side Processing & File Privacy

This is the cornerstone of our privacy policy. Vishnu Tools is a "client-side" application. This means that all the tools on this website run directly in your local web browser. Your files are NEVER uploaded, transferred, or stored on our servers or any third-party server. They remain on your computer at all times. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet after the page has loaded; the tools will continue to function.

2. Personal Information

We do not collect any personal information. You are not required to create an account, sign up, or provide any personal data like your name or email address to use our services.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

Vishnu Tools does not use cookies for tracking. We may use your browser's `localStorage` or `sessionStorage` for the sole purpose of improving your user experience (like saving your theme preference), but this data is stored on your device and is not accessible to us.
